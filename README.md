### 📖 About STEMIC
**STEMIC** is an audio asset exchange and creator ecosystem engineered specifically for music producers, sound designers, and independent sample labels. Addressing the high commissions and walled gardens of traditional sound marketplaces, STEMIC introduces a decentralized, creator-first digital economy.

#### Key Platform Innovations:
- **Disruptive Creator Economics ("Keep 100%" Model)**: A Pro Seller tier featuring **0% platform fees**, empowering sound designers to retain 100% of their digital catalog earnings.
- **Community Feedback Credit Economy**: An active participation loop where music producers earn marketplace credits by providing structured peer reviews and feedback on other creators' tracks.
- **Automated 3D Artwork Generator**: An integrated client-side tool that transforms 2D cover art into professional tilted 3D sample pack boxes dynamically.
- **Anti-Piracy Audio Watermarking**: Lightweight, compressed, watermarked audio previews that prevent unauthorized ripping while maintaining instant auditioning speeds.
- **Verifiable Digital Licensing**: Cryptographically signed and hashed PDF license certificates generated on-the-fly for every purchase.

### 🛠️ Technology Stack & Engineering Architecture
Engineered for real-time responsiveness, audio streaming, and high concurrency:

- **Frontend Core**: React 18, Vite
- **Motion & Dynamics**: Framer Motion & Anime.js audio visual motion primitives
- **Authentication & Realtime State**: Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore
- **Security & Bot Protection**: Cloudflare Turnstile Verification
- **Compute & Cloud Backend**: Google Cloud Run (Node.js/Express) scaling to zero
- **Database Architecture**: Google Cloud SQL (PostgreSQL) for transactional ledgers & Firebase for profile state
- **Global Edge & Media CDN**: Cloudflare Edge Caching paired with Google Cloud Storage (GCS)
